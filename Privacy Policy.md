# Vismap Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Vismap is a travel planning app provided by Workalisation ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how we handle information when you use Vismap, its Share Extension, widgets, and connected services.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: **teamlead@workalisation.dev**.

## Information we handle

**Accounts.** Vismap creates a guest account so that its online features can work without registration. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and the profile information those services make available, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.

**Places and travel plans.** We process places you save, coordinates, source links, descriptions, notes and other information you enter, lists, trip dates, accommodation details, routes, and visited-place marks. This information is stored on your device and in our backend to provide the app and synchronize your data.

**Links you import.** When you paste a link or share content with Vismap, we process the link and relevant accompanying text. To identify places, we may retrieve and process the linked page, captions, transcripts, images, or other content available from that source. Links can contain personal information or access tokens; avoid submitting private, confidential, or sensitive material that is unnecessary for your travel plan. Vismap does not need your social network password to import a link.

**Location.** If you grant location permission, Vismap uses your device location to show you on the map and support nearby search and related map features. Search areas, coordinates, and map requests may be sent to mapping or geocoding providers when needed for those features. Saved place and trip coordinates are part of your saved data. You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings; you can still use features that do not require your current location. Vismap does not require continuous background location tracking.

**Purchases.** Apple processes App Store payments. We and our subscription service provider, Adapty, process purchase and subscription information, such as product and transaction identifiers, subscription status, expiration dates, country, and account or profile identifiers, to deliver and restore Premium access, prevent abuse, and understand purchases. We do not receive your full payment card details.

**Technical and usage information.** Our services and providers may process IP addresses, app and operating system versions, device information, account and installation identifiers, diagnostic logs, crash reports, performance information, and feature or onboarding events. Sentry receives diagnostic information that may include your account identifier and email address. We use this information to operate, secure, debug, and improve Vismap.

**Device and tracking permissions.** We use Apple's DeviceCheck to help prevent repeated use of introductory free benefits. This can involve a device-generated token and an Apple-maintained eligibility flag. Where Vismap asks for tracking permission, Adapty may also receive your advertising identifier if you authorize it, for attribution and related analytics. You can refuse tracking or change your choice in iOS Settings without losing the app's core functionality.

**Support.** If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

## How and why we use information

We use information to provide accounts and synchronization; save and organize places; import links; search for places and generate travel plans; show maps, widgets, and sunset information; deliver and restore purchases; enforce usage limits and prevent abuse; respond to support and privacy requests; and maintain and improve reliability.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in maintaining and securing that service, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent where required, including relevant device permissions. You may withdraw consent through the corresponding settings or by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## Providers and other recipients

We use service providers for specific functions:

- **Supabase and our hosting providers:** authentication, database storage, synchronization, and backend processing.
- **Apple, Google, and Adapty:** sign-in, purchase processing and subscription management, and related analytics, as applicable to the features you use.
- **Sentry:** crash reporting, diagnostics, and performance monitoring.
- **AI and content processing providers:** including CometAPI and the AI models it makes available, and Supadata for supported source content and transcripts. Relevant imported content, place information, and trip context are sent to these services when needed to identify places, summarize content, or generate a plan. AI results may be inaccurate. These services have their own data handling terms; this policy does not promise that submitted content is never retained or used by a provider.
- **Map, place search, routing, and geocoding providers:** including Apple Maps, Google Maps/Places, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, and the map or navigation services you choose. Requests may include search text, coordinates, or route destinations.

We send providers information relevant to the function they perform. Source websites may receive ordinary web requests when Vismap loads their content. If you open an external navigator, website, sign-in page, support channel, or social app, that service's privacy policy also applies. We may use Telegram for support and internal operational notifications, such as purchase summaries.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and security, investigate abuse, or complete a business transfer subject to applicable privacy requirements.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Vismap keeps local data and caches on your device. Its Share Extension and widgets use an App Group shared container for pending links, widget snapshots, and related state. Information stored in that container is used by Vismap and its extensions.

We retain account and saved travel information while needed to provide the service. You can delete saved items in the app and delete your account using **Profile → Account → Delete account**. You may also contact us for help deleting guest data or making another privacy request. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data but does not by itself request deletion of backend records or cancel an App Store subscription.

After an account deletion request, we remove the account and associated application data from our active systems, subject to information we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution purposes. Logs, provider records, and backups may remain for their applicable retention periods and are not necessarily erased immediately. Data already sent to an external service may also require a request under that service's procedures.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data protection authority. Contact **teamlead@workalisation.dev** to make a request. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request concerns your account.

You can manage location and tracking permissions in iOS Settings, manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings, and choose which links and information you submit. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

## International processing and security

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required by applicable law, transfers must use the applicable legal safeguards. We use measures intended to protect information, including encrypted network connections where supported, account authentication, and access controls. No storage system or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children

Vismap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Higher local minimum ages or parental permission requirements may apply.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Vismap or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or request consent when required. For questions or requests, contact **teamlead@workalisation.dev**.
